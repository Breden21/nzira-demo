Try it in four steps
  1. Open CR-004. The check flags a long wait between hand-over and the lab.
  2. Open CR-006 or CR-011. The duplicate does not agree with the original.
  3. Switch to Geologist and open the Site plan. CR-008 sits outside the site boundary.
  4. Open any sample, choose "Alter a record (demo)", then "Verify record". The altered entry is caught.
What a pilot would measure
  1. Time from sampling to a result the miner can read, against today's paper process.
  2. Share of samples with a complete, verifiable custody record.
  3. Share of results that fail a quality check, and how many a geologist then clears.
  4. Cost per sample for the operator and the lab.

Checks in this demo are examples. Limits such as 10% duplicate difference and 48 hours between hand-over and lab receipt will be set with geologists and the laboratory.

Screening results are always shown as preliminary. A result is only called certified when it comes from a laboratory assay. Certification itself depends on the laboratory's own accreditation.
